About

I'm a master's student in computer science at UNC Chapel Hill, where I'm fortunate to work with Snigdha Chaturvedi and Katya Pertsova. Much of NLP treats disagreement as noise to be averaged away. I think it's often the most interesting part of the signal — whether an argument is fallacious, whether a label is right, whether two answers are really saying the same thing.

My current work spans fallacy detection and argument quality, predicting soft label distributions that preserve genuine annotator disagreement, and evaluating the semantic diversity of LLM outputs. Earlier, I co-led LRPlan, a multi-agent LLM + LRM planning framework published in Findings of EMNLP 2025. The longer version of all this lives on the research page.

Before UNC, I completed my B.Tech in computer science at IIT Delhi, where I worked with Mausam, and spent a research internship at POSTECH.

I am applying to PhD programs in NLP this fall, for admission in Fall 2027. If my work sounds relevant to your group, I'd love to hear from you.
