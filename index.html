About

I'm a master's student in computer science at UNC Chapel Hill, where I'm fortunate to work with Snigdha Chaturvedi and Katya Pertsova. My current work spans fallacy detection and argument quality, and evaluating and improving the semantic diversity of LLM outputs. Before UNC, I completed my B.Tech in computer science at IIT Delhi, where I worked with Mausam. I co-led LRPlan, a multi-agent LLM + LRM planning framework published in Findings of EMNLP 2025. I spent a research internship at POSTECH, South Korea, where I had the pleasure of learning under Hyung Ju Hwang.

I am applying to PhD programs in NLP this fall, for admission in Fall 2027.
